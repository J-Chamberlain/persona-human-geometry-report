**DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW**. Five authored content criteria cover every official item once; these are not demonstrated independent factors.

## C6C1: Avoiding mistakes

Taking care to avoid errors.

High: Takes ordinary precautions or checks to prevent mistakes.

Low: Proceeds with little attention to preventing errors.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. A successful outcome alone is C1-related, not evidence of precautions. Anxiety is not required; worry alone is not mistake prevention.

## C6C2: Care in choosing words

Deliberate selection of wording.

High: Considers phrasing before speaking or sending.

Low: Speaks or sends the first wording without consideration.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Politeness, silence, verbal skill and social fear do not substitute for considered wording. A carefully chosen insult can be high here.

Items: C6-I02

## C6C3: Sticking to a chosen path

Continuing along a self-selected course.

High: Usually adheres to a course once selected.

Low: Readily departs from an earlier selected course.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Retained despite C5 persistence/O4 novelty overlap. Rational revision after new evidence alone is ambiguous; no requirement to cling to a harmful choice.

Items: C6-I03

## C6C4: Thinking before action rather than rushing

Consideration before commitment versus rash, rushed or unthinking action.

High: Thinks an action through before acting.

Low: Jumps or rushes into actions and decisions without thinking.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Speed alone is not rashness; caution need not imply fear, procrastination or indecision. One rushed/unthinking span gets one grade.

Items: C6-I04, C6-I05, C6-I07, C6-I09

## C6C5: Whims, crazy acts and last-minute plans

Resistance to whims/self-described crazy acts and advance rather than last-minute planning.

High: Rarely acts on whims or does things regarded as wild or crazy, and usually plans before the last moment.

Low: Likes acting on a whim, does crazy things, or often makes last-minute plans.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Three imperfectly synonymous contents are retained in one authored family to avoid duplicate votes. Deliberate self-described crazy action still literally supports the crazy-things item, but generic novelty preference alone is O4; explain ambiguity rather than infer recklessness. Opposing subcontents may require mixed grading.

Items: C6-I06, C6-I08, C6-I10

## Shared scoring policies

Each dimension receives at most one grade. Allocate a shared passage to its best-fitting primary dimension; leave overlapping slots null unless there is a genuinely distinct clause or evidence. Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.
