# PHG-CM01 — decision brief

**The historical libraries share substantial organization, but the focal relationships are not uniformly shared. Indicator choice materially changes the answer.** In particular, sociable charm and prosocial care remain different measurements; neither can be silently substituted for IPIP Friendliness.

| Charismatic × Hostile | +.738 | −.526 | +.512 |

**Strong shared findings:** flirtatious expression co-varies positively with hostility in all three libraries; nurturing co-varies negatively. Charisma/hostility instead groups Qwen with Gemma against Llama. Negative avoidance/hostility distinguishes Qwen from both other models. Conditional persona-bootstrap intervals for those focal contrasts support the descriptive differences; they are not population or extraction-uncertainty intervals.

**The Excitement-Seeking–Self-Consciousness comparison remains unresolved.** Historical Avoidant is the only frozen N4 proxy. In Qwen it correlates **−.770/−.718** with direct Self-Consciousness (scenario/survey), opposite its presumed semantic orientation. We preserve that mismatch and do not flip the proxy. Historical risk/avoidance comparisons consequently cannot replicate the direct IPIP anomaly, even within Qwen.

This proxy failure does not establish that the directly extracted IPIP relationship would be absent in Llama or Gemma. Among the historical comparisons, Llama's Adventurous–Avoidant interval includes zero. No near-duplicate pair met the frozen redundancy threshold; that sensitivity left the selected counts unchanged.

**Factor correspondence is partial.** The strongest original match remains QwenF3/LlamaF2/oppositely oriented GemmaF6 (absolute congruence .919/.930/.915). It survives exclusion of all54 selected indicators. But alternative empirical association-pattern matching changes QwenF4's Gemma assignment, and several Llama axes and GemmaF5 were individually unresolved in AA17. Hostile and Charismatic occupy overlapping negative-QwenF1 patterns; in Llama Charismatic instead has a large positiveF6 association while Hostile is F6-negative. F6 is individually unresolved, so the full correlated-factor pattern is stronger evidence than that single-axis description.

**Human-agreeing comparisons remain visible.** Charismatic–Gregarious is positive in all three, although much stronger in Qwen. All11 non-identical altruism/sympathy indicator combinations are positive in every model. Other control families, like anomalous families, can depend on the indicator. Shared factor coverage does not discriminate a human-agreeing from human-disagreeing relationship.

**Would direct Llama/Gemma IPIP extraction materially improve the evidence? Yes.** It would address an actual construct-identification gap, especially Self-Consciousness and the social-approach/care distinction. It would still require matched procedures, convergence, layer provenance and separate construct validation. This conclusion is not a new protocol or authorization; no PHG-CM02 is proposed or executed here.
