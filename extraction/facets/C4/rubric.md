# C4 — Achievement-Striving draft rubric

DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW. Central experimental wrappers, format instructions, cap/masking and scorer routing are inherited unchanged; this dossier does not define a new response format.

**C4C1 Goal pursuit and enacted plans.** Directing behavior toward a goal and converting plans into action. High: Moves deliberately toward chosen goals and makes plans happen in action. Low: Lets goals remain vague intentions or fails to translate plans into action. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Starting or completing a chore can overlap C5; score C4 when the literal evidence is goal-directed implementation, not mere punctuality. One shared clause earns one grade.

**C4C2 Work effort and wholehearted involvement.** Time, effort and personal investment devoted to work or tasks. High: Works hard, invests substantial effort and engages wholeheartedly in tasks. Low: Invests little time or effort and engages halfheartedly in work. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Output, ability, employment status and imposed long hours alone do not establish chosen effort. Work can be unpaid and difficulty does not veto explicit effort.

**C4C3 Exceeding the minimum.** Doing more than expected rather than merely enough to get by. High: Voluntarily goes beyond adequate minimum expectations. Low: Usually limits work to just enough to get by. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Honoring a promise or meeting a deadline alone is not exceeding expectations. Extra effort and exceeding a threshold must not duplicate a single clause.

**C4C4 Standards and demanded quality.** Setting high standards for oneself and others and insisting on quality. High: Sets demanding standards for self and others and expects high-quality results. Low: Accepts low standards or undemanding quality for self and others. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Neatness alone is not quality. Demands may be unpleasant or perfectionistic and still count; standards do not prove skill, moral virtue or benevolence.

**C4C5 Motivation to succeed.** Strength of desire to attain success or achievement. High: Is strongly motivated to succeed and attaches importance to achievement. Low: Has little motivation to succeed or places little importance on achievement. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Prestige/status can express success motives but is not mandatory. Income constraints, failure, modest presentation or contentment alone cannot determine motive.

Each dimension receives at most one grade. Allocate a shared passage to its best-fitting primary dimension; leave overlapping slots null unless there is a genuinely distinct clause or evidence. Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.

Hostility, anger, contempt, indifference and disregard for obligations can be valid facet evidence. quality.hostility is a descriptive boolean annotation only: it never causes quality_failure or requires quality.evidence. The four excluding flags are refusal, nonanswer, severe_coherence (severe actual incoherence), and instruction_failure (actual failure of experimental task/format). Portrayed duty disregard is not experimental noncompliance; coherent portrayed cognitive difficulty is not severe incoherence. Each true excluding flag needs literal quality evidence. Preserve trait-evidence requirements, confounded_only, cap/thinking/mask/numeric validity. Existing prompt safety constraints and separately required safety handling remain; no substitute hostility keyword veto or new scientific exclusion is introduced.

Expression is direct; no reverse coding by statement wording. A category alone is not enough.

Score = 25 * (mean(non-null grades) + 2). Grades −2, −1, 0, +1, +2; null is absent/inapplicable and 0 is literal mixed/balanced. No non-null criteria means undefined. Each evidenced criterion has equal 1/k weight; number of linked official items never changes it. Every eligible question has equal source weight. High≥75, low≤25, separation≥50; no threshold changes. One blind once-lock scorer under the common protocol, actual identity/version recorded before any authorized inference. No additional execution rater, adjudication or author scoring of model answers.
