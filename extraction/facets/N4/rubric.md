## N4C1: Intimidation and standing up for oneself

Being easily intimidated versus able to stand up for oneself.

High: Feels cowed by another person or unable to speak in own defense.

Low: Can stand up for self without being readily intimidated.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. Leading others is E3, not required. Choosing a compromise without intimidation is ambiguous; literal ability to stand up for self is retained even without wanting to dominate.

## N4C2: Embarrassment and fear of a wrong move

Social embarrassment and fear of doing the wrong thing.

High: Feels readily embarrassed or fears a socially wrong action.

Low: Is not easily embarrassed and can act without strong fear of a wrong move.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. Moral conscientiousness, regretting real harm and general future worry do not alone establish social self-consciousness. Visible blushing is not required.

Items: N4-I02, N4-I07

## N4C3: Approaching others and friendship-dependent comfort

Difficulty approaching others and comfort restricted to friends.

High: Finds approach difficult or feels at ease only with established friends.

Low: Can approach unfamiliar people and feel comfortable before friendship is established.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. Low E1/E2 interest in contact or a preference for solitude alone is insufficient. Explicit approach difficulty still counts even if interest in company is strong.

Items: N4-I03, N4-I05

## N4C4: Fear of attention and speech stumbling

Fear of drawing attention and stumbling over one’s words.

High: Fears becoming the focus or reports stumbling over words in ordinary interaction.

Low: Can draw necessary attention without fear or speak without ordinarily stumbling.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. Speech stumbling is retained as direct official content, without claiming its cause. Mere accent, brevity, a single typo or known physical speech constraint alone is not a general pole. Avoid assigning the same attention-induced halting span twice.

Items: N4-I04, N4-I06

## N4C5: Unfamiliar and difficult social situations

Comfort in unfamiliar situations and being unbothered by difficult social situations, in the self-consciousness direction.

High: Feels awkward, exposed or unsettled in unfamiliar or socially difficult situations.

Low: Feels comfortable in unfamiliar situations and unbothered by ordinary social difficulty.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. O4 novelty preference and N6 helpless coping are insufficient. Literal unfamiliar comfort remains content even without an explicit embarrassment motive; a specific unsafe environment alone is ambiguous.

Items: N4-I08, N4-I09
