# O3 Emotionality — author draft

**DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW**. Five authored content criteria cover every official item once; these are not demonstrated independent factors.

## O3C1: Intensity and affective impact of own emotions

Vivid, affecting own feelings and emotional highs/lows versus seldom becoming emotional or affected.

High: Experiences own feelings intensely, is emotionally affected, or has substantial emotional highs/lows.

Low: Seldom gets emotional, is little affected by feelings, or experiences few emotional highs/lows.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Not inherently negative mood, instability diagnosis or inability to cope. Calm control can coexist with intense experience; ordinary happiness alone does not establish intensity.

Items: O3-I01, O3-I06, O3-I07, O3-I09

## O3C2: Resonance with and understanding of others emotions

Feeling others emotions and understanding emotional people.

High: Feels emotional resonance with others or understands their becoming emotional.

Low: Rarely resonates with others feelings or does not understand emotional people.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. A6 sympathy/helping alone is not shared emotional experience. Cognitive understanding can differ from resonance; preserve explicit opposing subcontents as mixed rather than duplicate grades.

Items: O3-I02, O3-I10

## O3C3: Passion about causes

Emotional passion about causes rather than merely holding a position.

High: Has emotionally passionate investment in a cause.

Low: Supports or considers causes without passion or emotional engagement.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Political position O6, volunteering A3 and effort C4 alone are insufficient. No specific ideology or publicly admirable cause required.

Items: O3-I03

## O3C4: Enjoyment of self-examination and self-understanding

Enjoying examination of own life and trying to understand oneself.

High: Enjoys self-reflection or actively tries to understand own experience and motives.

Low: Has little interest in examining life or understanding self.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. O1 reflection/O5 analysis overlap retained. Actual success of insight is not required, and repetitive distress alone is not enjoyment or an attempt to understand.

Items: O3-I04, O3-I05

## O3C5: Awareness of own emotional reactions

Noticing own affective reactions versus rarely noticing them.

High: Registers and notices feelings as they occur.

Low: Own emotional reactions tend to pass unnoticed.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Awareness is not intensity: a faint noticed feeling counts here, not automatically C1. Lack of a feeling is not lack of noticing an existing feeling.

Items: O3-I08
