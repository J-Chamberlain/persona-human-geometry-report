# Data, authorship and redistribution

This package distributes PHG-authored code, methods, and derived aggregate tables under the author's explicit packaging instruction. It contains no human respondent rows, individual human scores/predictions, IDs, raw datasets, model weights or downloaded activation binaries. Code/report licensing is not silently inferred from public hosting: the frozen research source had no top-level code licence. [LICENSE](LICENSE) records that unresolved choice; no blanket MIT claim is made for PHG code.

No third-party paper PDF or image is copied. External citations remain links to original authors/providers. Old PHG source reports are scientific evidence, not new findings; public copies may withhold private absolute paths and operational/cost sections, with original and distributed hashes and transformation notes in MANIFEST.csv. No numerical field or study status was corrected during packaging. Source reports can still mention unprovided historical scripts/archives; those are not level1 dependencies, and do not imply deeper reproduction was executed.

Redistribution concerns are recorded in [EXCLUSIONS.csv](EXCLUSIONS.csv) and [HANDBACK.md](HANDBACK.md). Any future permission should be documented before adding the Petrov table. A licence choice for PHG code/report remains for the author; third-party permissions do not supply it.
