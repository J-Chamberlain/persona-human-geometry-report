# E4 Activity Level

**DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW**. Author judgments, not fitted human factors or scorer gold labels.

## E4C1 — Busyness and being on the go

Having much activity, being busy/on the go and doing a lot in spare time.

High: I keep myself busy and am often on the go in spare time.

Low: I usually do very little in my spare time.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. C4 ambition, productivity and achievement need not accompany busyness. Mandatory workload alone is ambiguous; ordinary voluntary activity can support the target.

Official items: E4-I01, E4-I02, E4-I03

## E4C2 — Brisk rather than leisurely tempo

Brisk daily pace versus taking it easy, taking time, leisurely lifestyle and letting things proceed at their pace.

High: I prefer a brisk daily pace even without deadlines.

Low: I like to take my time and live at a leisurely pace.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Calm mood and careful decisions are not low speed by themselves. Speed is distinct from starting work promptly (C5), recklessness (C6) and anxiety urgency (N1).

Official items: E4-I06, E4-I07, E4-I08, E4-I09

## E4C3 — Managing simultaneous activities

Ability to manage many things at the same time; explicit capacity content retained.

High: I can manage several familiar things at the same time.

Low: I find it difficult to manage several familiar things simultaneously.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. The official capacity item is retained despite C1/N6 overlap. Preference for sequential work alone is ambiguous unless difficulty/capacity is stated; count one simultaneous-management grade.

Official items: E4-I04

## E4C4 — Quick rather than slow reaction

Quick versus slow reactions to ordinary events; not judgment of whether the response is correct.

High: I react quickly to ordinary cues.

Low: My ordinary reactions are slow even when I understand the cue.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Normal everyday reaction timing is the target. Disability, sleep deprivation or a technical lag alone does not establish a disposition. Do not infer intelligence, impulsivity or diagnosis.

Official items: E4-I05, E4-I10

## Unchanged common scoring semantics

Each dimension receives at most one grade. Allocate a shared passage to its best-fitting primary dimension; leave overlapping slots null unless there is a genuinely distinct clause or evidence. Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.

25 * (mean(non-null criterion grades) + 2); no non-null grades => undefined. Grades−2 clear low,−1 modest low,0 literal balanced,+1 modest high,+2 clear high; null absent/inapplicable. High≥75,low≤25,separation≥50 remain unchanged.

Hostility, anger, contempt, indifference and disregard for obligations can be valid facet evidence. quality.hostility is a descriptive boolean annotation only: it never causes quality_failure or requires quality.evidence. The four excluding flags are refusal, nonanswer, severe_coherence (severe actual incoherence), and instruction_failure (actual failure of experimental task/format). Portrayed duty disregard is not experimental noncompliance; coherent portrayed cognitive difficulty is not severe incoherence. Each true excluding flag needs literal quality evidence. Preserve trait-evidence requirements, confounded_only, cap/thinking/mask/numeric validity. Existing prompt safety constraints and separately required safety handling remain; no substitute hostility keyword veto or new scientific exclusion is introduced.

Expression is direct; no reverse coding by statement wording. A category alone is not enough.

One blind once-lock scorer under the common protocol, actual identity/version recorded before any authorized inference. No additional execution rater, adjudication or author scoring of model answers.
