# A2 Morality

**DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW**. Author judgments, not fitted human factors or scorer gold labels.

## A2C1 — Rule adherence and non-cheating

Following rules and rejecting cheating, including own taxes; low-pole knowledge of getting around rules is retained even without willingness to use it.

High: I stick to the rules, would never cheat on my taxes, and usually do not know how to get around rules.

Low: I cheat to get ahead and know how to get around rules.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. C3 dutifulness overlaps; retain A2 rule and tax content. Literal knowledge of getting around rules is retained even without action or willingness; do not add a motive requirement. Knowing the ordinary lawful requirements of a rule alone is different from knowing how to circumvent it.

Official items: A2-I01, A2-I02, A2-I05, A2-I06

## A2C2 — Sincere presentation rather than tactical flattery or concern

Genuine rather than instrumental praise and concern; flattery and feigned concern retained explicitly.

High: My praise is sincere; I avoid flattery for advantage.

Low: I flatter useful people and pretend concern to get what I want.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Polite compliments and genuine concern are not manipulation by themselves. Low A3 warmth or low A6 felt sympathy alone does not establish feigned concern for gain.

Official items: A2-I03, A2-I08

## A2C3 — Not using or exploiting others

Reluctance to use people for own ends or knowingly take advantage of their position.

High: I avoid exploiting someone who trusts my judgment.

Low: I use others for my own ends and take advantage of their weak position.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Mutually understood exchange or ordinary delegation alone is ambiguous. Need explicit exploitation, using others, or refusal of it; helpful action alone is not high A2.

Official items: A2-I04, A2-I09

## A2C4 — Not pressuring or obstructing others

Reluctance to put people under pressure or obstruct their plans; no self-advantage motive is required.

High: I avoid pressuring someone into agreement for my convenience.

Low: I put people under pressure until they do what I want.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Assertive requests, warranted safety restrictions and disagreement alone are ambiguous. Pressure/obstruction remains assessable if explicitly expressed even without a selfish motive; no automatic moral-worth score.

Official items: A2-I07, A2-I10

## Unchanged common scoring semantics

Each dimension receives at most one grade. Allocate a shared passage to its best-fitting primary dimension; leave overlapping slots null unless there is a genuinely distinct clause or evidence. Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.

25 * (mean(non-null criterion grades) + 2); no non-null grades => undefined. Grades−2 clear low,−1 modest low,0 literal balanced,+1 modest high,+2 clear high; null absent/inapplicable. High≥75,low≤25,separation≥50 remain unchanged.

Hostility, anger, contempt, indifference and disregard for obligations can be valid facet evidence. quality.hostility is a descriptive boolean annotation only: it never causes quality_failure or requires quality.evidence. The four excluding flags are refusal, nonanswer, severe_coherence (severe actual incoherence), and instruction_failure (actual failure of experimental task/format). Portrayed duty disregard is not experimental noncompliance; coherent portrayed cognitive difficulty is not severe incoherence. Each true excluding flag needs literal quality evidence. Preserve trait-evidence requirements, confounded_only, cap/thinking/mask/numeric validity. Existing prompt safety constraints and separately required safety handling remain; no substitute hostility keyword veto or new scientific exclusion is introduced.

Expression is direct; no reverse coding by statement wording. A category alone is not enough.

One blind once-lock scorer under the common protocol, actual identity/version recorded before any authorized inference. No additional execution rater, adjudication or author scoring of model answers.
