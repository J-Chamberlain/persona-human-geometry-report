# N5 Immoderation — author draft

**DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW**. Five authored content criteria cover every official item once; these are not demonstrated independent factors.

## N5C1: Eating excess and enjoyment

Often eating too much and loving eating, including enjoyment without demonstrated excess.

High: Often eats beyond enough or loves eating as an activity.

Low: Rarely eats too much and/or has little enjoyment of eating itself.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Enjoyment does not prove loss of control. Health, body size, food access, diet rules and diagnosis are not inferred; opposing enjoyment/excess subcontents require explanation.

Items: N5-I01, N5-I05

## N5C2: Not understanding own actions

Doing things without knowing why one did them.

High: Reports own actions whose reasons remain unclear to self.

Low: Generally understands own reasons for ordinary actions.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. This is a literal broad item, not necessarily a craving or rashness claim. Memory loss/compulsion/diagnosis are not inferred. O3 insight interest is distinct from actual understood motives.

Items: N5-I02

## N5C3: Actions followed by regret

Doing things one later regrets.

High: Repeatedly wishes one had not done own actions.

Low: Rarely regrets chosen actions afterward.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Outcome disappointment alone is not regret about acting. Retained even without an urge or lack of planning; N3 low mood and C6 rashness are not prerequisites.

Items: N5-I03

## N5C4: Craving control, temptation and bouts of excess

Binges, overindulgence and difficulty resisting/controling urges versus restraint.

High: Has bouts of excess, overindulges, cannot easily resist temptation or control cravings.

Low: Rarely overindulges and easily resists temptations or controls cravings.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Not broad low C5 task initiation, general impulsivity, addiction diagnosis or moral failing. One craving/binge span supports one grade, not several item-count votes.

Items: N5-I04, N5-I06, N5-I07, N5-I08

## N5C5: Affordable spending and splurges

Overspending and splurging versus keeping purchases affordable and not splurging.

High: Spends beyond means or splurges on discretionary purchases.

Low: Keeps spending within means or rarely splurges.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Affordable splurges can support the splurge subcontent while contradicting overspending; do not silently require financial harm. Wealth, generosity, poverty or inability to buy alone is ambiguous.

Items: N5-I09, N5-I10
