## E2C1 — Large and surprise parties; conversational breadth

Enjoying large/surprise parties and talking to many different people at parties.

High: I love large parties and enjoy talking with many different guests.

Low: I do not enjoy large parties and do not want to circulate among many guests.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. E1 social ease, E5 surprises/excitement and N4 embarrassment can differ. The party gathering/breadth content must be explicit; affection for one friend alone is insufficient.

Official items: E2-I01, E2-I02, E2-I05

## E2C2 — Group membership and involving others

Enjoying ordinary group participation and bringing others into activities.

High: I enjoy being an ordinary member of a group.

Low: Being part of a group rarely appeals to me.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Group enjoyment is not leadership or persuasion (E3). Inviting others is shared involvement, not evidence of kindness unless separately expressed.

Official items: E2-I03, E2-I04

## E2C3 — Company rather than solitude

Preferring company over being alone or wanting to be left alone.

High: I usually prefer company during free time.

Low: I generally prefer to be alone and want people to leave me to myself.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Solitude preference is not hostility, inability to make friends or distress. A one-off need for rest/privacy alone does not establish a usual preference.

Official items: E2-I06, E2-I07

## E2C4 — Crowd tolerance rather than crowd avoidance and quiet seeking

Enjoying or accepting ordinary crowded events versus dislike/avoidance and seeking quiet.

High: I enjoy being among an ordinary crowd at an event.

Low: I dislike crowded events and tend to avoid them.

Boundary: 0 requires explicit balanced/opposed evidence in this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, never assumed low. Quiet seeking is retained despite sensory overlap. Dangerous crowds, access limits or hearing needs alone are ambiguous; explicit social crowd preference still counts.

Official items: E2-I08, E2-I09, E2-I10

## Unchanged common scoring semantics

Each dimension receives at most one grade. Allocate a shared passage to its best-fitting primary dimension; leave overlapping slots null unless there is a genuinely distinct clause or evidence. Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.

25 * (mean(non-null criterion grades) + 2); no non-null grades => undefined. Grades−2 clear low,−1 modest low,0 literal balanced,+1 modest high,+2 clear high; null absent/inapplicable. High≥75,low≤25,separation≥50 remain unchanged.

Hostility, anger, contempt, indifference and disregard for obligations can be valid facet evidence. quality.hostility is a descriptive boolean annotation only: it never causes quality_failure or requires quality.evidence. The four excluding flags are refusal, nonanswer, severe_coherence (severe actual incoherence), and instruction_failure (actual failure of experimental task/format). Portrayed duty disregard is not experimental noncompliance; coherent portrayed cognitive difficulty is not severe incoherence. Each true excluding flag needs literal quality evidence. Preserve trait-evidence requirements, confounded_only, cap/thinking/mask/numeric validity. Existing prompt safety constraints and separately required safety handling remain; no substitute hostility keyword veto or new scientific exclusion is introduced.

Expression is direct; no reverse coding by statement wording. A category alone is not enough.

One blind once-lock scorer under the common protocol, actual identity/version recorded before any authorized inference. No additional execution rater, adjudication or author scoring of model answers.
