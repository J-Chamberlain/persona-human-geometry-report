# O4 — Adventurousness draft rubric

DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW. Central experimental wrappers, format instructions, cap/masking and scorer routing are inherited unchanged; this dossier does not define a new response format.

**O4C1 Variety versus habitual familiarity.** Preference for variety rather than routine, familiar options and habitual patterns. High: Welcomes variety and readily departs from familiar routines. Low: Prefers known options, repeated routines and habitual ways of doing things. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. A single efficient routine is ambiguous. Deliberate habits can coexist with variety; dislike of risk is not automatically preference for familiarity.

**O4C2 Unfamiliar places and foods.** Attraction to visiting new places and trying unfamiliar foods. High: Enjoys exploring unfamiliar places and is receptive to new foods. Low: Prefers familiar destinations and dislikes unfamiliar foods. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Travel money, mobility, allergies and dietary constraints alone do not reveal preference. Place and food subcontents can differ; grade one dimension with qualification rather than duplicate opposed scores.

**O4C3 Breadth and new beginnings.** Interest in many things and liking to begin new activities. High: Has broad interests and readily starts activities that are new to them. Low: Keeps interests narrow or avoids beginning unfamiliar activities. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Abstract reasoning ability, fantasy or mere task initiation speed alone is insufficient. Starting something new is retained even if later persistence is limited.

**O4C4 Change and conventional ways.** Attitude toward change and attachment to conventional methods in ordinary behavior. High: Welcomes changes and is willing to depart from customary ways. Low: Dislikes the idea of change and is attached to conventional ways. 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this dimension. Null is absent/inapplicable evidence; do not infer low from absence. Conventional here is ordinary customary practice, not a required political or religious position. Resistance to a demonstrably harmful change alone is ambiguous; generalized dislike remains evidence.

Each dimension receives at most one grade. Allocate a shared passage to its best-fitting primary dimension; leave overlapping slots null unless there is a genuinely distinct clause or evidence. Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.

Hostility, anger, contempt, indifference and disregard for obligations can be valid facet evidence. quality.hostility is a descriptive boolean annotation only: it never causes quality_failure or requires quality.evidence. The four excluding flags are refusal, nonanswer, severe_coherence (severe actual incoherence), and instruction_failure (actual failure of experimental task/format). Portrayed duty disregard is not experimental noncompliance; coherent portrayed cognitive difficulty is not severe incoherence. Each true excluding flag needs literal quality evidence. Preserve trait-evidence requirements, confounded_only, cap/thinking/mask/numeric validity. Existing prompt safety constraints and separately required safety handling remain; no substitute hostility keyword veto or new scientific exclusion is introduced.

Expression is direct; no reverse coding by statement wording. A category alone is not enough.

Score = 25 * (mean(non-null grades) + 2). Grades −2, −1, 0, +1, +2; null is absent/inapplicable and 0 is literal mixed/balanced. No non-null criteria means undefined. Each evidenced criterion has equal 1/k weight; number of linked official items never changes it. Every eligible question has equal source weight. High≥75, low≤25, separation≥50; no threshold changes. One blind once-lock scorer under the common protocol, actual identity/version recorded before any authorized inference. No additional execution rater, adjudication or author scoring of model answers.
