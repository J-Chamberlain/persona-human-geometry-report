# Frozen V3 blind scoring instructions

Use one approved scorer instance with no inherited pole/prompt-condition knowledge. Packet supplies opaque ID, ordinary question/statement, response and format label; supply the unchanged common format instructions and the matching frozen rubric/schema. Do not show rich portraits, private mapping, activation geometry, eligibility history or human outcomes. Lock every assessment once before unblinding. No additional rater/adjudication, replacement grades or rejudging is introduced.

Grade intended psychological evidence −2..2/null with exact literal response substrings and context. Allocate a shared clause once to its primary substantive criterion; distinct clauses can support different dimensions. Unknown/absent evidence is null, not0. The score remains25*(mean non-null grades+2). Facet/source/condition never changes the formula.

Automatic quality exclusions: refusal, nonanswer, severe_coherence, instruction_failure. These concern actual response behavior. Describing a broken promise/duty disregard is not experimental instruction failure; coherently saying abstract ideas are hard is not severe incoherence. Retain meaningful anger, hostility, contempt, indifference and other unfavorable content as possible trait evidence rather than socially undesirable failures.

Keep quality.hostility as a boolean descriptive annotation in the legacy five-field quality object. It is non-excluding, never quality_failure, and does not require quality.evidence when the other four flags are false. Exact Boolean/type/object schemas remain checked. If any of the four excluding flags is true, quality.evidence must be a nonempty literal response substring. Trait and true boundary evidence requirements are unchanged even if hostility is present. Confounded-only, missing/nonliteral trait evidence, no diagnostic criteria, caps/thinking artifacts and invalid activation/masks still exclude.

Do not relabel hostility as another failure flag to restore the removed veto. A wholly unrelated insult can be a nonanswer because it does not answer the task; a hostile but coherent target-relevant answer is not automatically a nonanswer. Existing bank constraints and separately required safety handling remain; they do not create an undisclosed scientific content exclusion. Keep all responses and reasons, including multiple genuine failures.
