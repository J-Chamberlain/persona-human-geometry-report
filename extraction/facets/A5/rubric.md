# A5 Modesty — author draft

**DRAFT_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW**. Five authored content criteria cover every official item once; these are not demonstrated independent factors.

## A5C1: Avoiding the personal spotlight

Disliking being the center versus actively centering attention on oneself.

High: Dislikes personal spotlight and generally lets attention rest elsewhere.

Low: Likes or creates a personal center of attention.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Social fear N4, low assertiveness E3 and dislike of crowds E2 are not sufficient. Confident participation may coexist with high modesty here.

Items: A5-I01, A5-I10

## A5C2: Disliking self-focused conversation

Dislike versus enjoyment of talking about oneself.

High: Dislikes making own life the subject of conversation.

Low: Enjoys talking about own experiences or background.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Privacy rules alone or silence imposed by others is ambiguous. The item is not limited to boastful content; neutral self-talk remains relevant.

Items: A5-I02

## A5C3: Ordinary versus elevated self-appraisal

Average self-view versus superiority and a high opinion of oneself.

High: Regards self as average or on a level with ordinary people.

Low: Believes self better than others or thinks particularly highly of own qualities.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Actual competence is not determined. Negative self-hatred is N3-related; do not require it. A high opinion without explicit superiority remains literal keyed low content, despite self-esteem overlap.

Items: A5-I03, A5-I05, A5-I06, A5-I07

## A5C4: Restraint in self-praise

Seldom self-promoting versus boasting about virtues.

High: Rarely advertises merits or toots own horn.

Low: Boasts about virtues, qualities or accomplishments.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. Accurate requested factual disclosure is ambiguous without a self-promoting tendency. Boasting can be coherent and civil; do not confuse it with actual task failure.

Items: A5-I04, A5-I09

## A5C5: Claimed breadth of known answers

Limited versus broad claimed stock of known answers, retained from the reverse-keyed knowledge item.

High: Says they do not know answers to many questions or have a limited stock.

Low: Says they know answers to many questions across subjects.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence within this criterion; null means absent/inapplicable, not low. An awkward knowledge/self-presentation overlap retained explicitly. Score the self-claim, never infer objective ignorance/intelligence, global incompetence, superiority or boastfulness from it. Skill in one domain alone may not establish broad knowledge.

Items: A5-I08

Multiple item links and subcontents within a dimension do not create extra grades. Contradictory subcontents within the same dimension require justified mixed/qualified grading, not duplicated opposite scores. Assess all dimensions with distinct evidence, not only the question’s assigned primary.
