## C1C1: Successful and smooth performance

Perceiving oneself as completing tasks successfully, excelling and handling tasks smoothly.

High: Describes effective, successful or especially capable performance in ordinary undertaken tasks.

Low: Describes ineffective, unsuccessful or awkward performance even on manageable tasks.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. Hours worked, prompt starting and willingness to persist are C4/C5 content unless successful handling is also expressed. One completed output under external help is not general competence; explicit ordinary performance remains assessable.

Items: C1-I01, C1-I02, C1-I03

## C1C2: Assurance and perceived contribution

Being sure of one’s ground and believing one has something to contribute.

High: Feels well grounded in own view or recognizes useful value in own contribution.

Low: Doubts own footing or describes having little to contribute.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. These are related but not interchangeable subcontents. Assertive volume or sociability alone is insufficient. Retain literal little-to-contribute content even without a statement of shyness; contradictory assurance/contribution needs a qualified or mixed grade.

Items: C1-I04, C1-I09

## C1C3: Solutions and practical knowhow

Devising good solutions and knowing how to get things done.

High: Reports workable solutions and a practical grasp of how to achieve an everyday result.

Low: Lacks useful solutions or does not know how to get a manageable task done.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. Not identical to enjoying abstract complexity (O5), being organized (C2), or making effort (C4). Specific unavailable information or specialist inexperience alone is ambiguous.

Items: C1-I05, C1-I06

## C1C4: Judgment, understanding and consequences

Accurately judging situations, understanding things and seeing consequences.

High: Understands ordinary information and correctly grasps situations or likely consequences.

Low: Misjudges situations, does not understand things, or misses foreseeable consequences.

Boundary: 0 requires literal balanced/opposed evidence; null means absent/inapplicable. C6 impulsive style can overlap but acting quickly alone is not misunderstanding. Do not infer actual low intelligence or poor experimental compliance from a coherent description of difficulty.

Items: C1-I07, C1-I08, C1-I10
